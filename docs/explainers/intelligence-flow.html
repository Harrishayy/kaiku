Every model, when Kaiku uses it, and through which API

Seven models and one path with no model at all. Her state and the crisis path are on the last row: rules, never a model.

What the gates hold back

  • Device gate. With her switch "Camera during activities" on (off by default), the phone or Mac counts face movement while a game or the check-in runs. Each frame is looked at in memory and let go. One record of numbers per task leaves the device; it has no field for a picture, face points, an identity or a feeling.
  • Home gate. Only observations (obs.*) and tool calls reach Kaiku's cloud. Raw sensor streams, audio and pictures do not. The cloud refuses anything else by type.
  • Live gate. Her voice, and pictures only if she switched them on for a walk, go straight from the device to Gemini Live with a token that works once, for one model, for five minutes. Kaiku's cloud is not on that path and stores no audio.
  • Music gate. The robot's drum game is the one other call that passes the cloud by: when music is switched on (it is off by default), the hub opens its own connection to Lyria RealTime and sends a style and a tempo. The style is one of five fixed phrases, picked by her mood dial; her name, her state and the dial number are not sent. The music comes back as sound and stays on the home network.
  • Model gate. The voice model gets a small card in plain words: her name, up to three notes she chose to tell Kaiku, her routines, her own ratings today, what was left open, how the last talk sounded, and a tone. Never the state, a score or watch data. Gemma gets clean facts for the companion (no digits, no state words, no health words) and structured evidence for the practitioner's review. Every answer is checked by code before it is used, and every paid call passes a spend cap first.
  • Care gate. Her practitioner sees evidence, the review and a graph of activities and routines. Her notebook appears as counts and themes only, and only if she shares it. Memories and transcripts are never read by a care route.

How this was measured

  • One simulated person. "Aino" is an authored record of sixteen days (every observation is marked sim: true), seeded through the real routes into a private copy of the cloud. Every payload on this page is a real output for that record, trimmed.
  • Real model calls, 14 Nov 2026. Three short Gemini Live conversations (five spoken turns) held by a test client standing in for the Mac and iPhone apps: the same session route, one-use token, Live connection and tool route, with a synthetic test voice. Then five questions to the mind, three voice renders and one practitioner review. Other timings are medians from the project's own call ledger of the same day; the count is given with each number.
  • What the voice chose. In those five turns Gemini Live called feel twice and recall three times, and never ask_mind. The mind's answers shown here come from questions we sent to the same tool route.
  • Not measured. Pictures from real glasses and the robot's hardware were not on the bench. Those hops say "not measured".
  • The drum game. One real run on 14 Nov 2026: real Lyria RealTime and the real hub, with the head's software twin. The taps came from the twin, not a person, and no flashed head, real speaker or real knock has been measured. In that run the hub had no cloud address, so the game's record waited in its queue at home. One spoken exchange with Gemini Live also ran through the twin's microphone and speaker: Kaiku's answer began 2.14 s after her last word.
  • Face signs. The record's 57 face records are authored, like the rest of it. No real camera or face has been measured: the apps ran on a stand-in source on the simulator. The gentler reply is from the evaluation's text stand-in for the voice (gemini-3.8-flash, one run per scenario, the cue set on a test card), not the spoken Live voice. On Aino's 16-day record the cue itself has not fired: it waits for an established usual. The compare and review times in that scene are from the reviews run while the feature was built; the count is given with each number.
  • Google's side. Gemini Live keeps conversation state for up to 24 hours so a dropped call can resume. That is the provider's retention, not a Kaiku store.
