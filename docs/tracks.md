# Track mapping

What each track asks for, what we show, and where it lives. Refreshed on 15 November 2026 from the code. The
depth is in [`stack/`](stack/INDEX.md), one page per partner technology: what we use and how, what has run with
dates and numbers, and whether it was the right choice. The dated requirement-by-requirement audit
of 14 November is [`track-readiness.md`](track-readiness.md) (a point-in-time page; where it disagrees with
this one, this one is newer). The briefs as text are in [`tracks/`](tracks/INDEX.md). The script is in
[`demo-narrative.md`](demo-narrative.md).

| Track (criteria) | What we show | Proof today | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aalto: Beyond the Clinic** (usefulness, ease of use, smart sensing, creativity; 25% each) | One person's day across robot, phone, Mac and glasses; four signal groups, each compared with her own baseline; a fixed consensus rule; a practitioner dashboard that shows who is worth a check-in and why; a learner that favours what she responded to. | The software loop runs end to end on a simulated record, live at the public URL. How the rule behaves on synthetic courses is measured in [`validation.md`](validation.md). No real-person data and no clinical calibration. | [`stack/INDEX.md`](stack/INDEX.md), `config/criteria.toml`, `cloud/kaiku_cloud/analytics/`, `apps/apple/`, `apps/web/` |
| **Best Use of Gemini API** (problem, integration, innovation, why Gemini) | Gemini Live is Kaiku's one voice on robot, phone, Mac and glasses. Short-lived tokens renewed mid-call; TTS in the same voice; embeddings; Lyria RealTime for the drum game. | Real provider calls on every path: 2.3 s to first sound, a 4 min 48 s call across a token renewal, tools, barge-in, pictures as a walk's view. No model sets a state. | Verified through the hosted API (`gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`) and, for the review and a record question, on our own vLLM on a Modal H100 (15 Nov).
