# AI Glasses for Everyone, Everyday (Meta) — main challenge

Brief as given on the Junction 2026 challenge page, copied 15 November 2026.

## The challenge
Demonstrate how a first-person field of view enables new or better interactions in everyday scenarios.

## Introduction
AI glasses from Meta offer a first-person view that can make everyday interactions more useful, hands-free, and in
the moment. At Junction, we invite you to explore where this perspective creates value beyond simply mirroring a
phone screen. Help us turn a clear everyday need into an intuitive, inclusive experience for people with all
abilities.

Build the best everyday use case using AI glasses from Meta, showing how a first-person field of view enables new
or better interactions in everyday scenarios.

Best entries will clearly articulate the problem and intended audience, and deliver a functional prototype and deck
to materialize their concepts. Creativity, technical execution, real-world impact, and inclusive interaction design
for people with all abilities will be key criteria.

Both display and audio-first experiences will be considered on equal footing, as will web apps and Device Access
Toolkit mobile integrations.

Show an original, relevant use case with a clear wearable benefit and a focused, plausible path to adoption or a
pilot, backed by a functional prototype that handles permissions, errors, performance trade-offs, connectivity, and
device state changes.

Interactions should be intuitive and inclusive for people with all abilities, with appropriate consent and safe
data practices within the demo's scope.

## Insight
We are interested in focused everyday problems where wearables unlock hands-free, in-the-moment value beyond basic
mirroring. Show a plausible path to adoption or a pilot, and make accessibility, appropriate consent, and safe data
practices part of the demo.

Teams with the best submissions may be invited to join Meta on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, for an AI forum where
leading industry professionals from various companies will explore the future of wearable AI.

## Resources
- 10 × Meta Ray-Ban Display and 10 × RBM Gen 2 for use during the hackathon, with charging and hygiene materials.
- Developer support at the Meta booth, with a challenge mentor as the point of contact.
- Mentors for web apps, for DAT on iOS, and for product marketing and DAT on Android.

## Judging criteria
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Creativity and use case | 25% |
| Technical execution | 25% |
| Real-world value and feasibility | 25% |
| User experience: input, accessibility and trust | 25% |

## What the entry must contain (from the text above)
- A clear problem and intended audience.
- A functional prototype **and a deck**.
- A clear wearable benefit beyond mirroring a phone.
- A plausible path to adoption or a pilot.
- Handling of permissions, errors, performance trade-offs, connectivity and device state changes.
- Accessibility, consent and safe data practices shown in the demo.

## Prizes
Best overall: each member of the winning team receives a pair of Ray-Ban Meta glasses.
