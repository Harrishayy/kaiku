# Best use of Arduino UNO Q (Arduino) — tech challenge

Brief as given on the Junction 2026 challenge page, copied 15 November 2026.

## The challenge
Bring your idea to life in the physical world with Edge AI and the Arduino UNO Q.

Use the Arduino UNO Q and Modulino sensors in your hackathon project, whatever challenge you are solving. Bring
Physical AI to life: run AI models directly on the device, sense the real world and make things move, light up or
react.

This tech challenge is open to all teams, so it can be combined with any other Junction challenge or your own idea.
Start by asking what problem you are solving and who it helps, then think about how hardware and Edge AI can make
your solution tangible. The best projects combine a working physical prototype with a smart use of on-device AI.

## Insight
Arduino also hosts the main challenge "The Green European Renaissance". This tech challenge is open to everyone:
the award goes to the team that makes the best and most creative use of Arduino in their project, in any challenge.

## Submission: two places
To be eligible for prizes the project must be **published on the Arduino Project Hub** as well as submitted on the
Junction platform, where peer review and judging take place.

1. Log in at https://projecthub.arduino.cc/
2. Click "+ NEW PROJECT".
3. Select the tag **Junction Finland 2026**.
4. Fill in the required parts.

## Hardware and software
- Kits are borrowed from the Arduino Hardware Lab (booking: https://2026.hackjunction.com/hardware).
- Arduino UNO Q (2 GB RAM, 16 GB eMMC), USB-C cable, USB-C hub (8 in 1), 45 W USB-C power supply, Arduino USB
  camera, Modulino Buzzer, Modulino Knob and Modulino Thermo.
- Software: Arduino App Lab. Bring a laptop with it installed.
- Mentors at the Arduino Hardware Lab and on Discord.

## Judging criteria
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Use of Arduino hardware and Edge AI | 50% |
| Working prototype and demo | 30% |
| Creativity | 20% |

## Prizes
Each member of the winning team receives an Arduino UNO Q and an Arduino Plug and Make Kit.
