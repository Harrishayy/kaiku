# Best Use of Gemma (Google DeepMind) — tech challenge

Brief as given on the Junction 2026 challenge page, copied 15 November 2026.

## The challenge
Awarded to the team that demonstrates the most innovative and impactful use of Google's Gemma open models. We're
looking for creative applications, strong technical execution, and solutions that showcase the potential of open AI
models to solve real-world problems.

## How to access Gemma
Gemma is Google's family of open AI models, available for developers to build with. It can be accessed and run
through several platforms, including the Junction 2026 partners:

- Featherless AI: hosted Gemma models through an API.
- Modal: deploy and run Gemma on serverless GPU infrastructure.
- Verda: cloud GPU resources to deploy and run Gemma yourself.
- Google AI Studio: experiment with Gemma in the browser.
- Hugging Face: download Gemma models and integrate them.

Setup instructions are in the official Gemma documentation. Event-specific credits or access instructions are in
the Junction Hackerpack.

## Judging
No weighted criteria are published. The brief names three things: an innovative and impactful use, strong technical
execution, and open models solving a real-world problem.

## Prizes
1,000 € per team for the top 3 teams, with no ranking between winners.
