# Best Use of Gemini API (Google DeepMind) — tech challenge

Brief as given on the Junction 2026 challenge page, copied 15 November 2026.

## The challenge
Build something extraordinary with Google's Gemini API.

This is an open innovation challenge: there are no predefined problems or industries to focus on. Whether you're
building an AI agent, a developer tool, a creative application, or something entirely new, we want to see what you
can create with Gemini.

The challenge is to demonstrate a meaningful, innovative, and technically impressive use of Gemini. Show us why
Gemini matters to your solution and what makes your project stand out.

## Insight
We're looking for projects that go beyond simply adding an LLM to an application. Think about how Gemini's
capabilities can enable something genuinely new or significantly improve an existing solution.

Your submission should clearly explain:
1. What problem you're solving and why it matters.
2. How you've integrated the Gemini API.
3. What makes your use of Gemini innovative or technically impressive.
4. Why Gemini is an important part of your solution.

The challenge is open to all industries, use cases, and creative ideas.

## Resources
- Google AI Studio (API key, experiments), Gemini API documentation, Gemini API quickstart.
- Gemini API credits for up to 400 participating teams, requested through the organisers' form; eligible teams
  receive account instructions during the event.
- Participation and the Google-provided accounts are restricted to participants aged 18 or older.
- Support at the Google DeepMind booth; "Build with Google DeepMind" workshop on Friday.

## Judging
No weighted criteria are published. The four points under Insight are what the submission is read against.

## Prizes
1st: 4000 € plus $1,000 in Google Cloud credits per team member. 2nd: 2000 €. 3rd: 1000 €.
